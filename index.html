3. Applied conversion rules

Invoice and partyRows are grouped by invoice reference. LIMIT and LIMITED are equivalent; a trailing LIMIT is displayed as LIMITED.
Address / stateState names are trimmed, normalized to uppercase, and administrative suffixes such as “(New)” are removed to match the supplied report.
Invoice orderInvoice groups retain the order found in the raw SAP export. The sample reference uses a different manual order, and no reliable sort field identifies that sequence.
Vehicle numberRecognized Indian registration numbers are standardized with a hyphen before the last four digits, e.g. AP16TJ-5656.
Material standardizationSpacing and bracket variations are normalized, e.g. TMT BAR 8MM (BOF) → 8mm[BOF]. Different rates remain separate rows.
GST and valuesThe effective GST rate is inferred from Net Sales when available. This handles intra-state rows where IGST Rate is 0 but total GST is still 18%.
FOR freightFor Incoterms = FOR, Freight Inclusive is used as Freight Rate. Freight Amount = Freight Rate × total invoice quantity. Remarks: FOR/transporter.
Other transportSELF BY PARTY → EX BY PARTY. Any other transporter name → EX BY transporter name.
ValidationConflicting party, state, vehicle, transporter or FOR freight-rate values within one invoice are flagged instead of silently producing an unreliable report.
Compatibility: modern Chrome / Edge. The Excel formatting engine loads from jsDelivr, so an internet connection is required when opening the page. Keep the original raw workbook unchanged.
